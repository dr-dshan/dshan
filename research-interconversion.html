Electron-Magnon-Phonon Interconversion

Investigate how charge carriers, spin waves and lattice vibrations exchange energy and angular momentum.

Research Focus

Track how electrons, spin waves and lattice vibrations exchange energy and angular momentum.

Electrons, magnons and phonons

Follow charge flow, collective spin precession and lattice motion as three distinct channels.

Magnonic spin transfer

Use magnonic spin dissipation to probe how spin excitations can reverse a magnet.

Spin-lattice coupling

Test how strain and phonon scattering affect magnetic dynamics and spin transport.

Interactive Illustration

Impact

Charge, spin and lattice motion provide different ways to deliver and detect a signal. Identifying when they exchange energy or angular momentum can reveal useful routes for electrical or acoustic control and show where energy is lost during switching.

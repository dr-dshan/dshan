Efficient Write Channels for Next-Generation MRAM

Switch magnetic bits reliably with less write energy.

Research Focus

Follow the charge-to-torque path to design faster, lower-energy magnetic writes.

Fundamental Physics

Trace spin and orbital currents from generation to torque at the magnetic interface.

Materials Science

Tune channels and interfaces to deliver more torque per ampere while limiting resistive loss.

Device Demonstration

Write the free layer with a lateral pulse. Read its state through the tunnel barrier.

Application

Evaluate lower-energy embedded MRAM at matched retention, read margin and switching error.

Interactive Illustration

Impact

Writing is a major energy and reliability constraint for magnetic memory. Finding how charge is converted into usable torque can guide the choice of channel and interface, so a smaller write pulse can switch a bit reliably.

Probabilistic Computing

Use controllable fluctuations for sampling, inference and optimization.

Research Focus

Use tunable p-bits and their interactions to sample and search in hardware.

Stochastic MTJs and p-bits

Use thermal switching in low-barrier MTJs to generate binary samples. An input bias changes how often each state appears.

Networks that sample

Couple p-bits so their states influence one another. Use the resulting samples for probabilistic inference and optimization.

Interactive Illustration

Impact

Sampling is a core operation in probabilistic inference and optimization. Biasable p-bits provide a hardware source of samples, and programmable coupling can turn a network into a search engine for candidate solutions. Its value is measured by the quality of samples and solutions it delivers for a given time and energy budget.
